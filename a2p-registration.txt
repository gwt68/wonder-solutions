NOTES BEFORE RESUBMITTING ANYWHERE
================================================================
1. The HELP message is weak. It has no brand name and no contact route.
   Carriers increasingly want both. Suggested replacement:

   Wonder Solutions: community announcements. For help email
   info@wondersolutionsny.com. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to
   unsubscribe.

2. The original Twilio campaign was rejected twice:
   - 30909: opt-in / call-to-action insufficient
   - 30886: campaign description didn't match brand details
   Root cause the first time: edits made before submitting silently did not
   save. ALWAYS reopen the saved record and re-read it before submitting.

3. The description leads with the legal name (MOISE FUCHS) on purpose - the
   validator flagged a brand-name mismatch when it didn't. Keep that.

4. Consent is web-form-only. The earlier version mentioned a verbal opt-in
   path and that contributed to rejection. Do not reintroduce it.

5. signup.html, terms.html and privacy.html must all be live and reachable
   at the time of review. Check them before submitting.

6. BRAND TYPE MATTERS A LOT. This brand is registered as "Private
   corporation" with a legal name of MOISE FUCHS, and it came back Low
   Volume Standard with a T-Mobile ceiling of 2,000-200,000 segments/day.
   If you register at another provider as "Sole Proprietor" instead, the
   TCR treats it as a different brand class entirely - much lower
   throughput (often a few hundred segments/day to T-Mobile) and no
   ability to scale. Use whichever classification is actually correct for
   your entity, but know that answering differently than you did here will
   change your limits, not just your paperwork. If sole proprietor is the
   honest answer, check the throughput ceiling before you commit to the
   move - at ~28,000 segments/month it may still be fine, but find out
   first.
